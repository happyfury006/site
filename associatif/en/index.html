Episode IV

A NEW COMMITMENT

After years of silence, the padawan JSG entered the ISIMA temple to learn the ways of the computing Force: databases, data analysis and decision support. But the Force is not found only behind a screen.

To keep up with the hours of coding, the young padawan joined the school's student associations. A board member of the student union for a year, he looked after the logistics that keep the temple alive, and lent a hand with events and the treasury whenever his allies called. Between two missions, you can find him at the union's parties.

As treasurer of a welcome weekend, he kept the accounts of twenty thousand credits to welcome nearly one hundred and seventy young recruits, a five-year record, and left the next board a treasure of four thousand credits.

When the union's archives reached their limits, he joined the team rebuilding its website, so that every organisation can finally manage itself.

With his allies, he also founded his own faction, whose records he kept: Drezzing, which dresses the school in its colours and sells its creations in its own online shop.

For one semester, the padawan went on a mission to a distant planet, Timișoara, before returning to the temple for his final year.

Today, new missions await him. Here is the map of his galaxy…

The welcome weekend

Treasurer

€20,000 budget

~170 participants, a 5-year record

+€4,000 surplus

The welcome weekend greets the school's new students. I handled its finances from start to finish: quotes, payments and reports. I also took part in the logistics, then in running the weekend on site.

The weekend closed with a surplus of nearly €4,000. The next team thus has a real reserve fund in case of trouble, which was missing in previous years.

Managing a €20,000 budget rigorously, from quote to final report, and planning ahead: a good result does not serve just one year, it also protects the following ones.

Moon of the BDE

The redesign of the BDE website

Fullstack developer, team of 3 · ongoing

The site must meet a need that grows every year: automating the recurring tasks of BDE members. The redesign replaces the old site with a Django API and a React interface backed by PostgreSQL, with permissions that let each organisation manage itself.

  • Organisations module: members, positions, terms of office and hierarchy of departments and clubs
  • Permissions tied to the position held in the organisation, no longer to a global role
  • Secure account creation and persistent sessions

What I take from it

Rebuilding a tool others already use: taking over what exists, going further, and designing the new modules without breaking current usage during the transition.

Moon of Drezzing

The online shop

Front-end contributor, team of 2

A SvelteKit front end connected to Medusa, an open-source e-commerce solution that handles the catalogue, the cart and payments. We started from scratch, with no mock-up, and revised the architecture several times as the site took shape.

  • Collection and category pages, with pagination and filters
  • Header and side panels grouped into reusable components
  • Legal pages: legal notice, terms of sale, privacy

What I take from it

Moving forward with no model to follow: testing ideas directly on screen, and accepting to revise the architecture as the overall vision becomes clearer.
